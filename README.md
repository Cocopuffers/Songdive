🎵 SongDive

7 Rounds. 7 Songs. How deep does your music knowledge go?

SongDive is a music trivia game where players answer music-related prompts and try to find songs that other players are less likely to choose.

🎮 How to Play

Play through 7 music prompts.

Find a song that matches each prompt.

Try to think beyond the most obvious answers.

Earn points based on the rarity of your answers.

Finish with a final score and challenge your friends!

⭐ Scoring System

SongDive uses four scoring tiers:

Rarity Tier

Points

Common

25

Uncommon

40

Rare

65

Extremely Rare

90

The goal is to discover songs that fit the prompt but aren't the first songs everyone thinks of.

🛠️ Current Status

SongDive is an early prototype. Features and scoring are still being developed, and the current song database is for demonstration purposes.

Future versions aim to introduce a larger song database and scoring based on real player submissions.
